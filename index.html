About

Dr. Alan Smith

I've spent the last fourteen years teaching computer science — algorithms, distributed systems, and the slightly unfashionable art of writing programs that other humans can read. My research sits where systems meet human-computer interaction: how we build software that scales without becoming impossible to reason about.

This year I accepted a private lecturing residency overseas, based in Kyoto. Rather than pause my teaching, I've moved the whole thing online. Everything I'd normally do in a lecture theatre — the live seminars, the messy whiteboard derivations, the office-hours tangents — now happens in the classroom on this site. If anything, the chat box has made the quieter students louder, which I count as a win.

When I'm not lecturing you'll find me learning just enough Japanese to order coffee correctly, and failing to resist rewriting my lecture notes one more time.
